# Founder (Track.FOUNDER, `bo-` cards)

Starter cards (`b-…`, in `Deck.kt`) count as level 1: problem, problem journal, interviews, MVP, margin,
break-even, LTV:CAC, cash vs profit, when to raise, M-Pesa, runway.

## Level 1 · Rookie
Separate business and personal money; revenue vs profit.

## Level 2 · Hustler
Finding real demand: The Mom Test, compliments vs commitments, workarounds, pre-selling, the concierge MVP.
Money basics: cash vs profit with numbers, paying yourself a fixed salary, simple bookkeeping, keeping records for 5 years,
net profit, markup vs margin, fixed vs variable costs, what a business must pay you before you quit a job.

## Level 3 · Pro
Unit economics: CAC, contribution margin, LTV, payback period, break-even revenue. Pricing: price rises vs discounts,
value-based pricing. Cash: burn and runway, "default alive", cash conversion cycle, growth that eats working capital.

## Level 4 · Shark: setting up in Kenya
Setup order (KRA PIN → BRS → county permit → tax obligations and eTIMS → payroll), BRS fees, liability,
choosing a structure (sole/partnership/LLP/company), single-director companies, turnover tax (1.5%, Ksh 1M–25M),
the Ksh 5M VAT threshold, eTIMS and deductible expenses, bank loans, chamas and SACCOs, the Hustler Fund,
CMA crowdfunding, the funding ladder. All rate/fee cards are `timeSensitive`.

## Level 5 · Legend
Failure data (KNBS 2016, CB Insights 2021, US BLS), Andreessen on product-market fit, the Sean Ellis 40% test,
Suri & Jack on M-Pesa, case studies (Copia, Sendy, Twiga, Wasoko), Wasserman's founder's dilemma, the cost of a hire,
employment contracts and probation.

## Left out (could not verify for 2026)
Startup Bill tax incentives (not law as of mid-2025), the final outcome of the 2025 ESOP-tax proposal, Nairobi permit fee amounts,
the Hustler Fund SME product limits, exact SACCO lending multiples.
