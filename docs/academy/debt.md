# Debt (Track.DEBT)

Cards: `core/src/main/kotlin/com/morphlabs/morph/core/academy/deck/DebtCards.kt` (prefix `db-`), plus 8 level-1 starters (`d-…`) in `academy/Deck.kt`.

## Levels

1. **Rookie**: principal vs interest, what "flat" interest means, the real cost of an M-Shwari loan (7.5% fee plus 20% excise), why paying only the minimum keeps you in debt, debt spirals. Starters cover comparing loans by total yearly cost, Fuliza, Hustler Fund, good vs bad debt, avalanche, CRB basics and HELB.
2. **Hustler**: turning monthly and daily prices into yearly rates, flat vs reducing balance with Ksh numbers, upfront fees, the minimum-payment trap, HELB terms, the Ksh 1,000 CRB threshold and free first clearance certificate, the Employment Act two-thirds deduction cap, a test for good debt, and a `you` card on savings vs debt.
3. **Pro**: converting flat rates to true rates (about 2×), avalanche maths, debt-to-income, Kenya's three CRBs and the 30-day notice before listing, guarantor risk, SACCO deposit multipliers, logbook loans under MPSRA 2017, the real price of asset financing, chama loans, M-Shwari rollovers, and the KBA cost-of-credit site.
4. **Shark**: total cost of credit and APR, compounding, consolidation, refinancing break-even, variable-rate stress tests, DSCR, the 2016–2019 rate cap and its effect, DCP Regulations 2022 and the 2024 widening to non-deposit-taking lenders, the in duplum rule (s.44A), and how to negotiate with lenders.
5. **Legend**: the Insolvency Act 2015 (3-year discharge, no-asset procedure, voluntary arrangements), Richardson et al. 2013 on debt and mental health, Bharadwaj, Jack & Suri on M-Shwari, CBK and FinAccess default data, Fuliza scale, Gal & McShane 2012 on the snowball method, Mullainathan & Shafir's "Scarcity", murabaha, in duplum edge cases, and a multi-debt case study.
