# Earning (Hustle Up): card outline

Track `EARNING`, file `core/.../academy/deck/EarningCards.kt`, id prefix `ea-`. 61 cards.

## Level 1: Rookie (12)
Human capital as your biggest early asset, the two levers (earn vs cut), gross vs net pay, payslip deductions,
active vs passive income, side hustles, why skills set pay, income streams, real vs nominal raises, networks,
investing in earning power, and a `you` card on what one month of spending costs.

## Level 2: Hustler (12)
Hourly value of a salary, the true hourly rate of a side gig (travel and costs), profit vs revenue,
cost-plus and value-based pricing, billable hours, price rises vs lost customers, building a portfolio,
when to quit for a hustle, asking for a raise, overtime maths.

## Level 3: Pro (13)
BATNA, anchoring, comparing total packages, the job-switch premium, certification payback, pricing around
platform fees, tax on online and foreign-client income, Turnover Tax (1.5%), counteroffers, answering
"expected salary", Ajira Digital, dollar-income currency risk, time value vs penny-saving.

## Level 4: Shark (12)
2026 PAYE bands, the after-tax value of a Ksh 10,000 raise, marginal vs average tax, pension contributions as
a tax shield (Ksh 30,000/month), NSSF Year 4 rates, SHIF/Housing Levy deductibility, Employment Act rights
(leave, 28-day notice, redundancy pay, written contracts, probation), career capital, skill stacking.

## Level 5: Legend (12)
Mincer earnings function, returns to schooling (Psacharopoulos & Patrinos), Leibbrandt & List on negotiation,
Babcock & Laschever, Goldin's "greedy jobs", the 2025 switcher/stayer reversal, Kenya's online gig workforce
(Mastercard 2020), ILO 2021 on platforms, superstar economics (Rosen), signalling (Spence), the child penalty
(Kleven et al.), and a `you` card on saving raises.
