# Economy & markets (Big Picture)

Cards: `core/src/main/kotlin/com/morphlabs/morph/core/academy/deck/EconomyCards.kt` (prefix `ec-`, 60 cards).

## Level 1, Rookie
Inflation and how KNBS measures it (CPI), CBK's 5% ±2.5 target, supply and demand, demand-pull vs cost-push, interest rates, the CBR, GDP, why fuel moves everything, EPRA's monthly price caps, real vs nominal pay, recessions.

## Level 2, Hustler
Real return on an MMF after tax and inflation, rule of 70, what a CBR cut does, the 2026 CBR hold at 8.75%, the Oct 2026 fuel rise in shillings, food vs headline inflation, 2025 GDP growth (4.6%), GDP per person, inelastic fuel demand, opportunity cost, price ceilings, a `you` card on idle savings.

## Level 3, Pro
Reading exchange rates, the 2024 shilling slide and recovery, import-cost maths, how CBR changes reach loans (KESONIA + K) and MMFs, public debt (~Ksh 13T, ~70% of GDP), Eurobonds and the 2024 buyback, FX risk on debt, remittances (~USD 5bn), the trade gap, debt service vs revenue.

## Level 4, Shark
The MPC, the 2026 oil-driven pause and second-round effects, IMF programmes, Finance Bill 2024 and the protests, how banks create money, bond prices vs yields, crowding out (Feb 2024 infrastructure bond), the 2016–19 rate cap, twin deficits, the yield curve.

## Level 5, Legend
Zimbabwe 2008 (Hanke), its causes, Kenya's 1993 inflation and Goldenberg, the 2008 crisis, Friedman & Schwartz on the Great Depression, central bank independence (Alesina & Summers; Art. 231), Suri & Jack on M-Pesa, the 2014 GDP rebasing, CBK and the shilling in 1966, the 2014 Eurobond, the Phillips curve and stagflation.
