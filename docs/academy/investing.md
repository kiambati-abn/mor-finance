# Investing (Track.INVESTING, ids `iv-`)

8 starter cards (`i-...` in `Deck.kt`) plus 56 cards in `InvestingCards.kt`.

## Level 1 – Rookie
Starters (risk, diversification, horizon, fees, DCA, index funds, real return, Ponzi) plus: the three asset classes, what a share is, compounding, the rule of 72.

## Level 2 – Hustler
After-tax and after-inflation MMF returns (15% WHT, CPI 6.8%), time in the market, fee drag over 30 years, DCA average cost, T-bills and Oct 2026 rates, DhowCSD, emergency fund before shares, long-run returns of shares/bonds/bills, a `you` card on fees paid.

## Level 3 – Pro
ETFs on the NSE (Satrix MSCI World Feeder, NewGold), ETF vs unit trust, P/E, dividend yield and 5% WHT, yield traps, why bond prices fall when yields rise (worked example), tax-free infrastructure bonds, asset allocation, rebalancing maths, currency risk (worked example and shilling history), liquidity risk (Kenya Airways suspension 2020–2025).

## Level 4 – Shark
Duration and its use, DCF and the Gordon growth model, SPIVA, the behaviour gap, Kenya's VASP Act 2025, crypto and forex-signal scams, Cytonn High Yield Solutions, sequence risk, efficient markets, leverage.

## Level 5 – Legend
Markowitz 1952, Fama–French factors, Sharpe's arithmetic of active management, Bogle and the cost matters hypothesis, Buffett's bet, Barber & Odean 2000, Bessembinder 2018, Dimson–Marsh–Staunton, survivorship bias, Graham's Mr. Market, tulip mania, dot-com, Japan's Nikkei, 2008, Grossman–Stiglitz.
