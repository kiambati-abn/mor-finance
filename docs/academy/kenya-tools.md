# Kenya Tools (Track.KENYA_TOOLS)

Cards: `core/src/main/kotlin/com/morphlabs/morph/core/academy/deck/KenyaToolsCards.kt` (prefix `kt-`), plus 11 level-1 starters (`k-…`) in `academy/Deck.kt`.

## Levels

1. **Rookie**: M-Pesa products (Fuliza vs M-Shwari/KCB M-Pesa vs Ziidi), merry-go-round vs investment chamas, KDIC cover (Ksh 500,000). Starters cover MMFs, real MMF returns, Ziidi, T-bills, non-competitive bids, bonds, SACCOs, SASRA, NSE/CDS, NSSF and where an emergency fund should sit.
2. **Hustler**: how KDIC cover works across accounts and what it excludes, SACCO shares vs deposits and their returns, monthly MMF income, the 15% withholding tax on interest, CMA approval, merry-go-round maths, the 2026 NSSF limits (Ksh 9,000 / Ksh 108,000), SHIF 2.75%, the 1.5% housing levy, and a `you` card on where the emergency fund gap should sit.
3. **Pro**: DhowCSD minimums (Ksh 50,000 non-competitive), T-bill discount maths, T-bill vs MMF, tax-free infrastructure bonds, bond withholding rates, coupon maths, CDSC vs CBK CSD accounts, REITs (80% payout rule, Fahari's price history), unit trust types, rollover risk, and selling bonds early.
4. **Shark**: PAYE bands and a worked calculation, taxable pay, a full Ksh 100,000 payslip, the Ksh 30,000/month pension deduction and what it saves, IPPs, Finance Act 2025 pension exemptions, the mortgage interest deduction (Ksh 360,000/yr), insurance relief, and which regulator covers what (CBK, CMA, SASRA, IRA, RBA).
5. **Legend**: Suri & Jack (2016) on M-Pesa and poverty, M-Akiba (2017), the Imperial and Chase bank failures and what savers can learn, the DhowCSD history, how CBK auctions set rates, chama legal structures, and the shift of unit trust assets out of MMFs.

Note: the starter `k-tbill` says the usual T-bill minimum is Ksh 100,000. CBK's 2026 auction notices say Ksh 50,000 for non-competitive bids, which is what `kt-tbill-min` uses. The starter is worth updating.
