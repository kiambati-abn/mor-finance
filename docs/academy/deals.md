# Deals (Track.DEALS, `sh-` cards)

Starter cards (`s-…`, in `Deck.kt`) count as level 1: "Ksh 2M for 10%", profit multiples, the mama mboga pitch,
two-step dilution, royalty deals, red flags, how to judge a pitch.

## Level 1 · Rookie
What equity is, loans vs equity, pre- vs post-money, how shareholders actually get paid, what investors want.

## Level 2 · Hustler
Turning an ask into a valuation (pre and post), what % for how much, comparing offers, the real cost of equity vs a loan,
royalty pay-back maths, when equity beats debt, owner labour vs true profit, sweat equity, 51% vs 75% votes,
shareholders' agreements.

## Level 3 · Pro
Dilution maths (multi-round, value of a smaller slice), the option pool shuffle, revenue and EBITDA multiples,
enterprise vs equity value, P/E, why multiples differ, founder vesting (4 years, 1-year cliff) with a calculation,
cap tables, fully diluted ownership, Carta median dilution per round.

## Level 4 · Shark
SAFEs (cap, discount, post-money), convertible notes, liquidation preferences (non-participating vs participating, with numbers),
anti-dilution (full ratchet vs weighted average), pro-rata, board seats, drag/tag-along, due-diligence red flags,
whether term sheets bind, BATNA.

## Level 5 · Legend
Partech 2025 (US$4.1B; Kenya led with ~US$1.04B), the 2022→2024 downturn, the rise of debt, Briter's Big Four share,
Shark Tank (Ring, Scrub Daddy) and Dragons' Den (Reggae Reggae Sauce, Tangle Teezer), the VC power law (Correlation Ventures),
Kaplan & Strömberg (2003) on VC contracts, down rounds, two "would you invest?" cases, and one `you` card on angel sizing.

## Left out (could not verify)
Bombas' exact equity on Shark Tank, Kenya-specific stamp duty on share transfers, Kenya's Lions' Den deal terms.
