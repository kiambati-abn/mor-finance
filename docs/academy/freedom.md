# Freedom (Track.FREEDOM, ids `fr-`)

14 starter cards (`x-...` in `Deck.kt`, using a ×30 freedom number) plus 50 cards in `FreedomCards.kt`.

## Level 1 – Rookie
Starters (what freedom is, five stages, ×25 vs ×30, savings rate, assets, passive-income ladder, Coast FI, sequence buffer, lifestyle creep, human capital, three `you` cards) plus: passive vs active income, freedom as choice rather than stopping work.

## Level 2 – Hustler
Freedom number at ×30, how spending cuts shrink it, savings rate vs years to freedom (a ×30 version of the classic table), why savings rate beats income, Lean/Fat/Barista FI, Coast FI maths, multiple income streams, a first low-capital stream, splitting raises, a `you` card on net worth vs the number.

## Level 3 – Pro
Bengen 1994, Trinity 1998, withdrawal rate to multiple, Meng & Pfau 2011, why Morph uses ×30 for Kenya, real yield on MMFs, years-to-target calculation, pension tax relief (Ksh 30,000/month), NSSF Phase 4 (Feb 2026), tax-exempt pension exits, runway for founders, a `you` card on spending vs savings rate.

## Level 4 – Shark
Sequence-of-returns risk, the bucket strategy and how to refill it, Guyton–Klinger guardrails, drawdown order, inflation over a long retirement, healthcare (post-retirement medical fund relief), planning for "black tax" and its cost, rising-equity glidepath (Pfau & Kitces 2014), valuation (CAPE) and withdrawal rates, early retirement and longevity.

## Level 5 – Legend
Kenya's pension coverage (about 26.5%), what it means for you, the one-third/two-thirds rule, annuities and the annuity puzzle (Yaari 1965), Die With Zero (Perkins), ERN safe-withdrawal research, the life-cycle hypothesis (Modigliani), Bengen's newer SAFEMAX, long-run returns for planning, two Kenyan case studies, and what makes portfolios last.
