# Property (Shamba & Keys): card outline

Track `PROPERTY`, file `core/.../academy/deck/PropertyCards.kt`, id prefix `ho-`. 61 cards.

## Level 1: Rookie (12)
Rent vs buy myths, title deeds, the official search and Ardhisasa, freehold vs leasehold, a car as a cost,
survey maps, the Land Control Board, deposits and mortgages, the yearly costs of owning, and a `you` card:
emergency fund before land.

## Level 2: Hustler (12)
The land-buying steps in order, stamp duty (4% urban / 2% rural), car running costs and depreciation, the
one-third rent rule, sale agreements, the 6-month LCB deadline, beacons, spousal consent, gross yield,
chama land, ground rent.

## Level 3: Pro (12)
Price-to-rent ratio, mortgage payment maths, Monthly Rental Income tax (7.5%), net vs gross yield, land fraud
red flags, mother titles, off-plan risk, capital gains tax (15%), how much a rate gap costs, transaction costs,
stamp duty on the valuation, "rent and invest the difference".

## Level 4: Shark (13)
KMRC, the size of Kenya's mortgage market, mortgage interest relief (Ksh 360,000/yr), the Housing Levy and
Boma Yangu, sectional titles and service charges, REITs (I-REIT vs D-REIT), car import taxes and the 8-year
rule, NTSA logbook transfers, leverage.

## Level 5: Legend (12)
The Rate of Return on Everything (Jordà et al.), Kiyosaki's "house is not an asset" vs imputed rent, land's
opportunity cost, the Swynnerton Plan, the 2010 Constitution on land, the Land Registration Act 2012 and s. 26,
Dina Management v Mombasa County (2023), the Ndung'u Report, non-citizens and agricultural land, the 2025
jump in bad mortgages, and a `you` card on concentration in one house.
