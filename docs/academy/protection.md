# Insurance, scams & safety (Shield Up)

Cards: `core/src/main/kotlin/com/morphlabs/morph/core/academy/deck/ProtectionCards.kt` (prefix `pr-`, 60 cards).

## Level 1, Rookie
What insurance is, premium/cover/excess, the emergency fund as self-insurance, fake M-Pesa SMS, "wrong number" refunds, PIN safety, SIM swap, fake job fees, forwarding to 333, scam red flags, what's worth insuring.

## Level 2, Hustler
A `you` card on emergency fund vs target, excess vs premium trade-off, underinsurance and the average clause (with numbers), fake Fuliza-limit SMS, OTP safety, Ponzi vs real business, why small losses are poor value to insure, claim steps, checking IRA licences, phone lock-down.

## Level 3, Pro
SHA/SHIF replacing NHIF (Oct 2024), 2.75% contribution maths and the Ksh 300 minimum, SHA's three funds, term vs whole life, term-plus-invest trade-off, sizing life cover, motor third-party vs comprehensive, DECI 2007 pyramids, CBEX 2025, forex "account manager" scams.

## Level 4, Shark
Valid wills (s.11), intestacy (s.35), the cost of dying without a will, SACCO and pension nominees, the Policyholders Compensation Fund limit (Ksh 500,000 from 2026), complaining to the IRA, Data Protection Act fines and rights, WIBA, small-business cover, the VASP Act 2025.

## Level 5, Legend
Risk pooling, adverse selection (Akerlof; Rothschild & Stiglitz), moral hazard (Arrow; Pauly), why SHIF is mandatory, index insurance and basis risk, Kilimo Salama / ACRE, IBLI in Marsabit, Karlan et al. 2014, Cole et al. 2013, failed Kenyan insurers, KUSCCO, Madoff.
